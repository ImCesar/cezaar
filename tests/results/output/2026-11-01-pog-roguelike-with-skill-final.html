Whatever lands face up works for the slammer, whoever put it there

A single-player desktop roguelike against AI, played with mouse or controller. Both fighters' rosters are built into one stack; you aim a slammer, it drops, and the pogs that flip fire their effects at the slammer's owner. Turns alternate until someone is dead.

Charms: three slots

Small numbers, big habits.

Number-only items that fire only on the holder's own slam, so they can never help the other side.

bought in shops

Roster: eight slots

Add one pog or cut one, never both.

After each fight take one of three offered pogs into an empty slot, or remove one. Three copies merge into one stronger pog (numbers x1.5) and free two slots. An empty slot holds a Blank, so the tower is always 16 high.

the foe fields eight slots too

4 Reshuffle

Misses are not lost, just mixed in.

Everything that did not flip is shuffled into a fresh pile. When the pile runs low the discard is shuffled back in, and every Heal loses 1 point per reshuffle.

Heal 3 → 2 → 1, so shared healing fades

3 Resolve top-down

One at a time, so you can follow it.

Each effect pops its number in order, then one summary card shows the net change for both sides.

all within the 3 s turn budget

The dud rule

Nothing flipped, nothing earned.

A slam that flips nothing is a dud and costs the slammer 1 health, so a poison-laden stack cannot be stalled out.

dud = −1 health

The run map

Twelve choices to a boss.

About 12 nodes, branching, ending in a boss. Fight, elite, shop (charms and slammers), rest (heals 30%). Health carries over between fights.

40 to 50 minutes
