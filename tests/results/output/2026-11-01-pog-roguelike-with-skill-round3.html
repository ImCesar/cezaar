1. Aim and drop

You choose where. Physics chooses the rest.

Aim with mouse or stick and the slammer drops with real physics. Pogs that land face up fire for the slammer, whoever put them in the stack, and can help or hurt.

Guards against long animations: drop, pops and card share a 3 s turn budget.

2. Both rosters, one stack

Their weak pog is in your tower too.

Each side fields eight slots. Both are shuffled into one stack, always 16 high. An empty slot holds a Blank pog with no effect, so the height never changes.

Guards against an obvious best shot: every aim point cuts through both sides.

3. After a fight: take or remove

Every fix costs a slot or a pog.

Take one of three offered pogs into an empty slot, or remove one. Never both. Three copies of a pog merge into one stronger pog (numbers ×1.5) and free two slots.

Offer 3 · merge 3 to 1 · ×1.5 · placeholders.

4. Read the strip

Every pog, top to bottom, no surprises.

The strip beside the tower lists each pog with icon, value and contributor colour. The bracket shows how deep the chosen slammer usually reaches from the aim point.

Guards against unreadable outcomes. Reach is usual, never promised.

5. Resolve top-down

One at a time, so you can follow the fight.

Each flipped pog resolves in turn, top to bottom, with its number popping up, then a summary card. A slam that flips nothing is a dud and costs the slammer 1 health.

Guards against poison stalls. About 0.35 s per pop.

6. Reshuffle

The pile remembers.

Pogs that did not flip are shuffled into a fresh pile. When the pile runs low, the discard is shuffled back in. Every Heal loses 1 point each reshuffle.

Guards against shared healing prolonging fights.

7. Choose a slammer

Read their roster, then pick your tool.

Dome flips the top few, Ring is hollow and tips one side, Flat is broad and reaches deep. You own up to three and choose one before each fight, after seeing the opponent's roster.

Guards against an obvious best shot. Reach numbers are placeholders.

8. The run

Forty minutes, one health bar.

About 12 stops, branching, ending in a boss. Charms are number-only, three slots, and fire only on the holder's own slam. Shops sell charms and slammers.

Guards against conversion items erasing trade-offs.
