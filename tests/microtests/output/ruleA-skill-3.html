Verdict for the producer

The claim is right on the count and wrong on the cause. The pick itself is the loss: it spends one of nine choices on nothing. Undoing spends a second, to win back a slot that is never short. In this model undoing is the worse play. And at nine choices with three-copy merges there is no spare choice, so one stray pick costs the whole third merge.

Where the run length lands

Merge arithmetic

merged = ceil(1.5 x n)

Rounding up favours small numbers: a 1 doubles, a 10 gains half. Merging three pieces into one yields x1.5, so a merge is a trade of three picks for one piece and two spare slots.

What these lanes assume

Every offer holds the build piece. The build is three pieces of three copies each. Blank counts as an empty slot you can take into. X does nothing for you in a fight. Merged pieces do not merge again.
