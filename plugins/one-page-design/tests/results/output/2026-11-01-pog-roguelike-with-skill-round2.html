One shared tower of pogs: each slam fires whatever lands face up

Single-player roguelike for desktop, mouse or controller, against AI opponents. Numbers are placeholders.

A TURN, IN FIVE MOMENTS

1 Aim

The tower sits still. You do not.

Move the slammer over the tower with mouse or stick. The strip's bracket shows how deep this slammer usually reaches from that point.

pick 1 of your 3 slammers before the fight

2 Drop

Real physics, real mess.

The slammer falls and the tower scatters. Pogs landing face up flip. If nothing flips, the slam is a dud and costs the slammer 1 health.

dud cost: 1 health

3 Resolve

Top down, one at a time.

Each flipped pog fires for the slammer, whoever contributed it, for good or ill. A number pops up per effect, then a summary card.

charms fire only on the holder's own slam

4 Sort

Winners out, the rest reshuffled.

Flipped pogs go to the discard. Everything else is shuffled into a fresh pile. When the pile runs low, the discard is shuffled back in.

every Heal loses 1 point per reshuffle

5 Swap

Turns alternate.

The other combatant slams the same tower. Play goes on until one combatant is dead.

opponents also field 8 slots

FThe 3 second budget

AObvious best shot

One shot that always wins.

Three slammers reach differently and you pick after seeing their roster. The tower is reshuffled every slam, so no row stays best.

1 slammer per fight

BUnreadable outcomes

You cannot see what just happened.

The strip lists every pog top to bottom. Effects resolve one at a time with a number each, then a summary card.

icon, value, contributor colour

CShared healing

Both sides heal, nobody dies.

Heals lose 1 point each reshuffle until they are worth nothing.

Heal 3, 2, 1, 0

DPoison stalls

Slam nothing, take nothing.

A slam that flips nothing is a dud and costs 1 health.

dud cost: 1 health

EConversion items

A charm that rewrites a trade-off.

Charms are number-only: they change amounts, never what an effect does. Three slots, holder's slam only.

charm slots: 3

FLong animations

Watching beats playing.

A 3 second budget per turn: drop, effects, card. Effects speed up when many flip.

turn budget: 3 s
