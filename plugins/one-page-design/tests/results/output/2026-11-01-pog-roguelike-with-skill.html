One shared tower, one slam: every pog that lands face up works for the slammer

A single-player roguelike on desktop (mouse or controller) against AI opponents. Both rosters are stacked into one 16-pog tower; you drop a slammer on it with real physics, and whoever reaches zero health first loses.

Turns alternate until one side is dead

Roster: eight slots each

Both rosters are shuffled into one stack. An empty slot is a Blank, so the tower is always 16 high.

After each fight

Take one of three offered pogs into an empty slot, or remove one.

Three copies merge

The merged pog is stronger (numbers x1.5) and frees two slots.

Charms

Three slots, numbers only. They fire only on the holder's own slam.

Risks the design answers
AObvious best shot. Three slammers reach differently, you pick after seeing their roster, and the pile reshuffles every slam.
BUnreadable outcomes. Tower plus strip show every pog; effects fire one at a time with a number, then a summary card.
CShared healing drags on. Heals lose 1 point each reshuffle: 3, 2, 1, gone.
DPoison stalls. A slam that flips nothing is a dud and costs 1 health.
EConversion items erase trade-offs. Charms are number-only, so none can turn a cost into a gain.
FLong animations. 3 s turn budget: drop, effects and card all fit inside it.
The run

About 12 nodes across a branching map, ending in a boss. 40 to 50 minutes. Health carries over between fights.
